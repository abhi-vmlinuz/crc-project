3 · Division (sender side) —

step 0 / 0
Same shape as the long division you learned in school — quotient on top, one staggered subtraction row per step. The only difference: "subtract" means XOR, because in binary arithmetic without carries subtraction and XOR are the same thing (never any borrows). Play walks through it automatically; Prev / Next (or the slider) move one step at a time.

4 · Channel + receiver —

data bit CRC bit flipped by the channel tip: click any bit to flip it

How to explain it: append n−1 zeros to the data (n = generator length), divide by the generator with XOR, the leftover is the CRC. Send data + CRC. The receiver divides what arrives by the same generator — remainder zero means no error. One flipped bit makes the remainder non-zero.
